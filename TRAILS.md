# Coral Trails

After you finish **3 tasks** on a coding path, a coral trail opens.

## What you do

1. Swim up the reef.
2. Earn **coins** at each stop (for the Fish Shop).
3. **Very rarely**, you might also get a **new fish you do not already have**.
4. Keep going to the next tasks when you finish.

## Files

- `trail.js` — when the trail opens and coin / rare-fish rewards
- `trail.css` — how the trail looks

Each language path has its own reef colors. Normal stops give coins only. Have fun diving!
