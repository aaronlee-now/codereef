# Coral Trails

After you finish **3 tasks** on a coding path, a coral trail opens.

## What you do

1. Swim up the reef.
2. Pick up prizes along the way.
3. Keep going to the next tasks when you finish.

## Files

- `trail.js` — when the trail opens and what prizes you get
- `trail.css` — how the trail looks

Each language path has its own reef colors and prize names. Have fun diving!
